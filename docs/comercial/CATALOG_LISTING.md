# Borrador del listado del Project Catalog

Fecha: 2026-09-16
Estado: borrador. El nombre y la categoria del listado se deciden el jueves 17/09 con dato de busqueda del Project Catalog, y hasta entonces quedan como hueco.

Reglas que cumple este borrador:

- El eje es hasta tres tipos de cartel con todas sus variantes, no cantidad de familias de producto (D15).
- El precio es el mismo de la landing: USD 250 de setup mas USD 29 por mes, presentado como piso (D17). No hay tiers ni precios de add-ons.
- No se usa la palabra gratis ni ninguna promesa de prueba (D25).
- Ingles nivel B2, frases simples.

## Nombre y categoria

Pendiente del jueves 17/09. Candidato de trabajo, sin cerrar:

> You will get an interactive 3D sign configurator with live pricing and quote capture

Upwork antepone "You will get", asi que el titulo no lo repite.

## Descripcion

> Your customers ask how much for a sign, and you answer one message at a time. This tool answers on your own page.
>
> The visitor picks the sign type, the size, the material, the lighting and the quantity. A 3D preview changes while they pick. The price updates on screen as a range, with your own note next to it. When they are ready, they send you the request by WhatsApp or by form, with every option already filled in, and they can print the quote sheet.
>
> The page carries your logo, your colors and your texts. Your prices are loaded and checked with you before it goes online.
>
> What you get covers up to three sign types, with all their options: face lit and back lit panels, totems, and channel letters with the text your customer types. It is not a template with one product in it.

## Que incluye

> - Quote tool with your logo, your colors and your texts.

> - Price on screen as a range, with your own note.
